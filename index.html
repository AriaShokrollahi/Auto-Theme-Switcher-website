About

Auto Theme Switcher 1.2

Version1.2
Installed in%LocalAppData%\Programs\AutoThemeSwitcher
Settings stored inWindows registry (current user only)

Auto Theme Switcher switches Windows between Light and Dark mode at the times you choose, and can change your wallpaper at the same time. It also provides desktop clock widgets. It runs entirely on your PC: no internet access and no data is collected.

Interactive preview: open each section and try the switches.

Automatic schedules

Set the time for light and dark mode, even across midnight. If your PC was off, the right theme is applied at startup.

Matching wallpapers

Choose one wallpaper for light and one for dark. They change at the same moment as the theme, with your favorite fit.

Desktop widgets

Six live clocks for your desktop. Drag them anywhere, or pin them so clicks pass straight through.

Drag the slider. This is what the app does for you, every single day.

Questions, answered

Lightweight. Privacy-friendly. Runs silently in the background.

Does it need administrator rights?

No. It installs for your user only and never changes anything system-wide.

Does it send any data?

No. It has no internet access and no telemetry. Your settings stay in your own user registry.

What if I switch the theme myself?

It only acts at the scheduled times, so your manual change stays until the next switch.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

The installer isn’t code-signed yet. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
